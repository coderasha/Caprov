CAPROV Real Estate Valuation Model

Current valuation mark selection and forward-mark projection · Model: caprov-forward-mark-v1

What this model is: a transparent, deterministic projection from a source-backed current valuation mark and operating facts.
What it is not: an independent appraisal, market quote, DCF, or guarantee of future value.

1. End-to-end flow

2. Current mark: evidence-led selection

The local intelligence pipeline extracts candidate facts from uploaded documents, keeps source provenance and confidence, and selects the current mark in this priority order:

PrioritySelected factModel method label
1Market value / fair valueIndependent valuation memo / appraisal extract
2NAVFund NAV statement extract
3Purchase priceAcquisition price from purchase agreement (no later mark available)
4No explicit valueClass heuristic pending primary valuation memo

Insurance, replacement-cost and book-value figures are explicitly not used as market marks when a market value is available. When more than one source corroborates a mark, the model records that in the valuation notes.

3. Real-estate forward projection

Projection requires a positive current mark. For real estate, the starting annual growth rate is 2.5%. The model applies the following deterministic adjustments, then clamps the result to the range −8.0% to +10.0% per annum.

DriverAdjustment formulaLimit
Occupancy(occupancy % − 90) × 0.0008−1.5 pp to +1.5 pp
WALT / WALE(lease years − 5) × 0.0015−1.0 pp to +2.0 pp
Cap rate(6 − cap rate %) × 0.002−1.5 pp to +1.5 pp
Purchase-price drift15% × annualized return of current mark versus purchase price−1.0 pp to +1.5 pp
Risk ratingHIGH −2.0 pp; ELEVATED −1.2 pp; MODERATE −0.5 pp; otherwise 0Not capped separately
annualGrowth = clamp(2.5% + occupancyAdj + leaseAdj + capRateAdj + purchaseDriftAdj − riskPenalty, −8.0%, +10.0%)

4. Scenario marks and confidence

For each horizon (1, 3 and 5 years), the system compounds the selected current mark:

base = currentMark × (1 + annualGrowth)^years
bear = currentMark × (1 + annualGrowth − 2.0%)^years
bull = currentMark × (1 + annualGrowth + 2.0%)^years

The displayed low/high range is centred on the base projection: ±8.5% at year 1, ±13.5% at year 3, and ±18.5% at year 5. Base confidence begins from the current-mark confidence, reduced to 85%; it receives an additional reduction for HIGH risk and declines by 8 percentage points per projected year, bounded between 25% and 85%.

5. Controls, provenance and downstream use

  • Every extracted fact carries document provenance; the projection retains source IDs for market value, NAV or purchase-price facts.
  • A missing or non-positive current mark prevents projection generation.
  • The model preserves the original valuation method and valuation date as the base-mark context.
  • Marketplace ask-price defaults, collateral pledged value and lending LTV workflows may consume the latest Asset DNA valuation mark.
  • Risk flags use document coverage, ownership evidence, jurisdiction, occupancy and WALT/WALE inputs; the resulting risk rating directly affects the projection.
