# CAPROV Product Summary

CAPROV is an AI-first private-asset intelligence platform for firms that manage complex, document-heavy alternative assets. Its core idea is **Asset DNA**: a versioned intelligence envelope that turns source documents like title deeds, SPAs, valuation memos, KYC packs, and financial statements into structured facts with provenance, confidence scoring, valuation, risk, and timeline context.

The updated platform now also creates a **trusted internal intelligence layer** by versioning each DNA rebuild, hashing the snapshot content, and recording provenance anchors for supporting documents and the snapshot itself.

The product combines three layers into one operating system:

- **Intelligence layer**: document ingest, classification, fact extraction, Asset DNA generation, valuation marks, risk scoring, forward projections, and a copilot that answers questions using DNA-backed context.
- **Operating platform**: organization management, users, role-based access control, audit trail, assets, documents, and portfolios.
- **Operating platform**: organization management, approval-gated onboarding, users, role-based access control, audit trail, assets, documents, and portfolios.
- **Capital markets workflows**: marketplace listings, trading orders, settlement, tokenization, collateral management, and lending built on top of DNA-backed asset records.

## What makes CAPROV different

- **Versioned Asset DNA** keeps a historical record of each intelligence rebuild instead of replacing prior snapshots.
- **Provenance-first extraction** links facts back to source documents and fragments so operators can review why a value or ownership claim exists.
- **Trusted snapshot verification** adds canonical hashing and internal version history for each Asset DNA rebuild.
- **Tamper-resistant provenance** can anchor evidence records through Ethereum Sepolia while preserving the internal source-of-truth workflow.
- **Deterministic core intelligence** keeps extraction, valuation, and risk logic stable and auditable.
- **Optional LLM assistance** improves copilot responses and can help fill extraction gaps, while the authoritative mark generation stays grounded in the deterministic pipeline.
- **Workflow continuity** means intelligence is not isolated; it directly supports downstream actions like portfolio management, listings, settlement, tokenization, and lending.

## Current product capabilities

### Intelligence

- Ingest asset-linked documents
- Upload PDF and DOCX files and extract structured facts into tabular review views
- Build versioned Asset DNA snapshots
- Hash and retain trusted DNA snapshot versions with provenance anchors
- Generate valuation, risk, and forward projection views
- Support cited copilot briefings over asset context

### Core platform

- Multi-user organization workspace
- Platform-admin approval of new organization access requests
- Role-based access control for admins, analysts, compliance, and viewers
- Asset and document management
- Portfolio views and audit logging

### Capital markets

- Marketplace listings
- Trading and order workflows
- Settlement lifecycle tracking
- Tokenization on Ethereum Sepolia in simulated or live modes
- Collateral and lending flows based on asset intelligence

## Ideal user

CAPROV is best suited for private-market operators, investment teams, asset managers, structured finance teams, and control functions that need a reliable way to turn fragmented asset paperwork into an actionable, auditable operating record.

## Short positioning statement

CAPROV helps firms convert private-asset documents into trusted, versioned intelligence and use that intelligence across operating and capital-markets workflows.
